Vantage is a curated learning platform and AI match coach for
Valorant — aimed at Gold to Ascendant players in the EU. This is
post #1.

## Why build it

The space isn't empty — Blitz, Tracker, Mobalytics, ProGuides all
exist. But they either drown you in stats without telling you what
to do about them, or gate good advice behind paywalled content
that's rarely tied to your actual play.

I want two things done well:

1. **Curated learning content.** A small pool of trusted creators,
   dynamically matched to your rank and your current focus — not a
   wiki, not a 300-video playlist.
2. **AI match analysis grounded in your data,** that ends with
   concrete next steps — and links each suggestion back to a lesson
   in the library.

Free to learn. Paid for the coach. No overlays. No scrapers. Riot's
official API only.

## The shape of the bet

- Solo-built in Switzerland.
- Four hours of dev a week, two hours of content curation.
- MVP in roughly seven to nine months — realistic, not aspirational.
- Build-in-public from day one, because marketing budget is zero.

That means scope discipline is the entire game. The MVP is locked:
five launch topics, Tier-1 AI match coach on manual-input (Riot
production approval is best-effort), Free tier plus one paid Coach
tier. Everything else is explicitly V1.1+.
